Hue ramps

Step 500 is the brand value of each hue. Step 800 exists almost entirely to back a badge in dark mode.

The gray ramp inverts

Gray is positional, not absolute: 100 is always the step nearest the background and 600 the step nearest the text. Switching themes flips the actual hex values. Never hardcode one.

Rules worth keeping

  • Green and red mean direction, never decoration. If it's green here, it went up.
  • Never encode direction with color alone. The ▲ / ▼ glyph is part of the component.
  • Orange is the only accent. Links, primary buttons, active tab, focus ring — nothing else competes, and nothing blue is clickable.
  • Don't hardcode a gray. The ramp inverts between themes; the token already knows which way is up.
  • Elevation is earned. Shadow means the thing floats. Rows don't float.
  • text-caption is not for anything load-bearing — it sits near 2.9:1 on a light ground.
